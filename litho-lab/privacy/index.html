Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Litho Lab (iPhone, iPad and Mac) is an educational optics simulator by Chuang. It collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics, and all calculations run on your device. The app makes no network requests.

Data kept on your device

Your experiment settings and experiments you save are stored on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on "Sync experiments with iCloud" in Settings (off by default), saved experiment files (.litholab) are kept in your own iCloud Drive through Apple's iCloud Documents. The developer receives nothing and cannot access them. You can turn it off at any time; the files are then managed under your iCloud settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing, files and links

If you share an image, movie or experiment, the system share sheet sends it only to the destination you choose. On the Mac the app is sandboxed: it can open only its own container and files you pick, and it has no network access. Optional links to educational sources and the email link open outside the app and follow their own privacy practices.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: luchuangl@gmail.com.
