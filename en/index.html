Learning notes

Every big idea starts
with a small question.

Short notes on what I’m learning right now.

optics
OPTICS

Why do tiny patterns blur?

Light travels as a wave. Lenses, apertures and diffraction decide how much fine detail survives into the image.

chips
CHIPS

How does light “draw” a circuit?

Lithography shines patterned light onto a light-sensitive coating, copying a circuit design onto a silicon wafer.

computing
COMPUTATION

Why does AI need so much computing?

An AI model is a huge pile of arithmetic. Chips packed with tiny circuits work through it, again and again, very fast.

words
WORDS

Why review a word just as you forget it?

Memories fade. Meeting a word again right when it starts to slip helps it last a little longer each time.
