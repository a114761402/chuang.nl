A LITTLE SCIENCE, EVERY DAY

It starts with a small question.

Notes from my learning journey

OPTICS

Why do patterns look blurry?

Light behaves as a wave. Lenses, apertures and diffraction all affect how much detail an image can reveal.

CHIPS

How does light “draw” a circuit?

Lithography transfers patterns onto light-sensitive material. It is a key step in making chips.

COMPUTATION

Does AI need computation?

AI models run on computing hardware. Tiny circuits work together to perform enormous numbers of calculations.
